Privacy Policy - Clifford Coaching Social Engine

Last updated: September 2026

Clifford Coaching Social Engine ("the System") is an internal content automation tool built and operated solely by Clifford Coaching (owner: Kristian Clifford) to plan, produce, and publish social media content on behalf of Clifford Coaching's own accounts. It is not a public product, software-as-a-service, or third-party platform. It is not offered to, and does not accept connections from, any organization or individual other than Clifford Coaching itself.

What the System connects to

The System connects, via each platform's own official API and OAuth authorization flow, to social media accounts owned and operated by Clifford Coaching, currently or in the future including YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram. Each connection is authorized directly by the account owner (Kristian Clifford) through that platform's own login and consent screen.

What data is processed

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the connected accounts, encrypted at rest and used solely to authenticate API requests made by the System on behalf of the account owner.
  • Content the System itself generates or schedules (drafts, captions, hashtags, and associated media) prior to and after publishing.
  • Public engagement metrics made available by the connected platforms about the account owner's own posts (e.g. views, likes, comments), used to inform future content decisions.

The System does not collect, process, or store personal data belonging to any third party (such as followers, commenters, or message senders) beyond what the connected platforms' own APIs return as part of normal engagement data on the account owner's own content.

Data retention and sharing

Tokens and content data are retained only for as long as the corresponding platform connection remains active, and are deleted or replaced when a connection is reauthorized or revoked. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. Data is not used for advertising or profiling.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the System can be directed to kristian@cliffordcoaching.no.
